Ten habits worth keeping

  1. Set a deposit limit before the first payment.
  2. Decide the amount first and treat it as the cost of a night out.
  3. Do not chase a loss. The next price does not care about the last one.
  4. Do not stake borrowed money, including credit, overdrafts or a friend’s cash.
  5. Set a time limit and switch reality checks on.
  6. Do not gamble to get away from stress, low mood or boredom.
  7. Do not gamble while drinking, tired or upset.
  8. Keep it visible to people close to you. Hiding it is a warning.
  9. Use a time-out of a day, a week or a month when the pull grows.
  10. Remember the margin. Over time the book keeps a share of stakes. No system removes it.

Warning signs

  • Staking more, or for longer, than you planned
  • Going back to recover money
  • Borrowing, selling things, or missing bills to fund it
  • Lying about the time or the money
  • Feeling restless when you cannot gamble
  • Using it to dull stress or low mood
  • Rows at home, or work and study slipping

If several of these fit, call 0808 8020 133.

Children and other people

Nobody under 18 may gamble in Great Britain. If a child uses a device you own, use filtering software, a separate account, and never leave a funded bookmaker session signed in.

If you are worried about someone else, the helpline advises relatives as well. Do not take on their debts alone. Gam-Anon supports people close to someone who gambles.

If an operator lets you down

Complain to the operator first. If it is still open after eight weeks, use the free dispute service named on their site. You can also report a concern to the Gambling Commission.
